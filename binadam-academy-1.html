FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do I need any experience to start?
No. Every course starts from the basics and builds up step by step. If you can use a smartphone, you can begin today.
Can I learn using only my phone?
Yes. Our lessons are mobile-friendly, and many students complete full courses on a phone. A laptop helps for web development and video editing but isn't required to start.
How do I pay?
We support bank transfer, card and popular mobile payment options. Send us a message and we'll share the best method for your country.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes. You'll receive a certificate of completion for every course you finish, and Pro and Business learners also get portfolio guidance.
How long do I keep access?
Course purchases include lifetime access to recordings and future updates for that course.
Is there a refund policy?
If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days and have completed less than 20% of the course, contact us for a refund. Edit this policy to match your terms.
